# Luxe Creative Studio

Creative Brief – Portfolio Website for EXTACYOFCRYPTO

I want a portfolio website that feels like an experience, not a normal website.

When someone lands on it, I want them to feel like they just entered a premium creative studio or digital art gallery. I don’t want a template. I don’t want a website that simply scrolls from top to bottom with static sections. I want the entire experience to feel alive.

The style should be modern, elegant, cinematic, and minimal. Think of the attention to detail you see in Apple’s product pages, luxury automotive websites, and award-winning creative portfolios. Every animation should feel intentional. Nothing should move just for the sake of moving.

The website should be built around my creative identity:

EXTACYOFCRYPTO

Motion Designer

Video Editor

Graphic Designer

The design should immediately communicate quality and professionalism before visitors even look at my work.

⸻

First Impression

When the website loads, I don’t want visitors to immediately see the homepage.

Instead, I want a short loading animation.

The background should be almost black.

My name, EXTACYOFCRYPTO, should slowly appear in a metallic or brushed gold finish with soft lighting.

After a few seconds, the letters should smoothly transform into the homepage.

The animation should feel luxurious rather than flashy.

⸻

Overall Look

The website should use a dark cinematic theme.

The primary colors should be rich black, charcoal, dark slate, and warm white.

Accent colors should be subtle and sophisticated rather than bright neon.

Use deep navy blue, muted teal, brushed gold, muted purple, and terracotta only where necessary to highlight important elements.

I want the interface to feel timeless.

No rainbow gradients.

No gaming aesthetic.

No cyberpunk overload.

The colors should make my portfolio feel premium and mature.

⸻

Hero Section

The homepage should immediately introduce who I am.

Display my name prominently:

EXTACYOFCRYPTO

Below it:

Motion Designer

Video Editor

Graphic Designer

Add one strong headline that communicates what I do.

Something similar to:

“I create visuals that capture attention and tell unforgettable stories.”

Instead of using a flat background, create depth.

The background should contain subtle floating creative elements like cameras, lenses, editing timelines, design grids, typography, and abstract geometric shapes.

Everything should move slowly and naturally.

The movement should be elegant, not distracting.

The visitor should immediately understand that they’re looking at the portfolio of someone who works with motion and visual storytelling.

⸻

Scrolling Experience

I don’t want the page to feel like people are scrolling through sections.

I want it to feel like the camera is moving through different rooms of the same creative studio.

Each section should naturally flow into the next.

Transitions should feel cinematic.

No sudden jumps.

No harsh cuts.

The website should guide visitors through a visual journey.

⸻

About Me

The About section should feel personal but premium.

Instead of placing a large paragraph in the center of the page, create an elegant layout with my image, a short introduction, and animated statistics.

The statistics should count upward as they appear.

Examples include:

Projects Completed

Clients Worked With

Videos Edited

Designs Created

Years of Experience

The section should communicate credibility without feeling crowded.

⸻

Portfolio

This is the most important part of the website.

I want my work to be organized instead of placed in one giant gallery.

Separate everything into categories.

Motion Design

Video Editing

Graphic Design

Branding

Social Media

Each category should have its own visual identity while still matching the overall design.

Projects should appear as premium cards rather than plain rectangles.

Hovering over a project should create a subtle animation.

Videos should preview automatically on hover.

Images should smoothly enlarge when selected.

Every project page should include:

Project title

Project overview

Creative objective

Tools used

My role

Final outcome

Optional client testimonial

I don’t want people to simply watch my work.

I want them to understand how I think and how I solve creative problems.

⸻

Creative Process

I want to show how I work from beginning to end.

Create a visual timeline that explains:

Discovery

Research

Planning

Design

Animation

Editing

Final Delivery

Instead of just displaying text, animate each stage as visitors scroll.

⸻

Skills

Don’t use progress bars.

Instead, create an elegant interactive section showing the software I use.

Adobe Premiere Pro

After Effects

Photoshop

Illustrator

Blender

Figma

When someone selects a tool, briefly explain how I use it and display projects created with it.

⸻

Testimonials

Client reviews should feel luxurious.

Instead of placing reviews inside plain boxes, use elegant glass or brushed-metal cards with subtle depth and lighting.

The testimonials should slowly move as if they are floating.

Visitors should immediately feel that real people trust my work.

⸻

Behind the Scenes

I also want a section showing my creative process.

Include my workspace, editing setup, work in progress, sketches, storyboards, experiments, and timelapse videos.

This should make clients feel connected to the work behind the final result.

⸻

Contact

The final section should leave a strong impression.

Display a bold message inviting visitors to work with me.

Include a professional contact form.

Display my social links.

The call-to-action should feel confident and memorable.

After submitting the form, the animation should feel satisfying rather than generic.

⸻

Motion

Animations should be smooth and premium.

Buttons should react naturally.

Cards should gently tilt when hovered.

The cursor should feel interactive.

Scrolling should feel fluid.

Nothing should move too quickly.

Every movement should reinforce the feeling of quality.

⸻

Performance

Although I want a visually impressive website, performance is extremely important.

The website must load quickly.

Animations should never reduce usability.

Videos should be optimized.

Images should be compressed without sacrificing quality.

The website should work perfectly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

The mobile version should not simply shrink the desktop layout.

It should be thoughtfully redesigned for smaller screens while preserving the same premium experience.

⸻

Final Goal

When someone visits my portfolio, I want them to think:

“This person clearly understands design.”

“This feels different from every other portfolio.”

“If this much attention went into their own website, I can trust them with my brand.”

The website should not try to impress people with excessive effects.

It should impress them through excellent craftsmanship, refined motion, thoughtful layouts, and an unforgettable user experience.

Build something that feels expensive, timeless, and worthy of representing a professional creative brand.
